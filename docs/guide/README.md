# Toolroll guides

Toolroll runs coding agents and business processes on your own
computer, with a person in charge of every decision that matters.

- [Getting started](getting-started.md): install, sign in, first project, starter kits, first flow
- [Flows](flows.md): zones, cards, triggers, templates, people and insights
- [Steps that reach outside](steps-outside.md): Jev, Claude drafts, web requests, email and tools
- [Code in flows](code.md): your own scripts in Python, Node or shell, as steps and as triggers
- [Subagents](subagents.md): your lead's named helpers, each deciding and handling cards within rules you write
- [Chat and your phone](chat-and-phone.md): the lead, Telegram buttons, Slack, Discord and Teams
- [Security](security.md): approvals, secrets, and what agents can't touch
- [Linux](linux.md): install, the agent fence, keeping it running
- [Troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md): what the common messages mean
